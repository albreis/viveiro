Profile generic-demo@5, established nest. The screening window is an exploratory choice within catalog bounds, not a biological confidence range. All inputs and ranges are model hypotheses/approximations, not calibrated uncertainties. Sigma combines nonlinearity/interactions and residual noise. Four trajectories do not give calibration precision; zeros may indicate an inactive mechanism in this scenario.

We retain inputs, responses per replicate, CSVs/manifests per World and indices for every parameter, including those outside the top ten. Replicates are averaged per point before computing each elementary effect. Missing data remain missing.

Interpreting the top ten

The top ten is a screening order of 12 inputs, not the discovery of ten nonzero effects. In this run, only five inputs changed final population. Zero ties are ordered by identifier, without scientific priority. P3.2 must also consider other metrics, horizons and real data before choosing adjustments.

Method and reproduction

EE = (Y_after − Y_before)/(x_after − x_before), with x normalized to [0,1]. µ is the mean effect, µ* the mean absolute effect and sigma the sample standard deviation (n−1). Each input has four effects after averaging two paired replicates per point.

Vanilla Morris, even grid, step p/[2(p-1)], one input per step. Seeds are shared between points of one trajectory/replicate; other trajectories/replicates receive other seeds. This implementation has no Campolongo optimization or confidence bootstrap.

Primary references: Morris 1991, SALib sampling and SALib analysis. Viveiro uses its own design RNG; it does not promise NumPy-identical samples.

npm run sensitivity -- --out nova-pasta --jobs 4 reproduces the default protocol. --resume checks existing files and source identity before reuse. Identical model, version, source, plan and seeds are required.

Raw data from this study

Files were generated with the source identified above. Future source edits must use a different directory and preserve this study.
