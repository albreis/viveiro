Teacher’s guide

Six guides for Brazilian upper-secondary education, connecting Biology and Physics within Natural Sciences. Initial technical version: teacher review pending. Times are planning suggestions, without a classroom pilot. The curriculum associations below are the authors’ proposals, not MEC endorsement.

Start here

  1. Open the laboratory. Preserve important work by exporting its JSON.
  2. Choose a lesson under One-click experiments. Switching saves a named checkpoint of the previous terrarium and opens paused A/B worlds; if saving fails, switching does not occur.
  3. Record a hypothesis before resuming. A/B advance together; later interventions affect both. Always compare the same model day.
  4. To accelerate, pause and use Advance without rendering, with the same destination for the pair. An exhausted budget gives a partial state, not the requested day.
  5. Record seed, version, scenario, day, measurements and limitations; preserve checkpoints or export JSON. When finished, restore Antes da aula.

Each displayed day belongs to the accelerated model, not a real species. Prepared recipes do not reconstruct colony history. Pore water is not relative air humidity.

Formative assessment and review

Ask for a one-page record: hypothesis, controlled comparison, table or drawing with day and unit, a supported conclusion and one model limitation. Suggested rubric, 0–2 per item: testable hypothesis; comparable record; data-supported argument; recognition of limitations. Do not reward only agreement with the teacher’s hypothesis. Results without differences count.

For review, use the review form: six forms have no submitted opinion. Real Biology and Physics teachers are needed; no review, SUS, pre/post-test or educational validation has occurred. Send suggestions without student data.

Prepare an offline lesson

In the public laboratory, open a version and choose Prepare for offline use. Wait for Ready to open offline before leaving. Reopen that version’s address; external catalogs and links still require a connection. Compatible browsers offer app installation in their menu.

Checkpoints stay local to the same browser and origin. Exporting JSON transfers a copy to another origin. Remove offline copy removes only this version’s files, preserving checkpoints. Browsers can reclaim storage; local copies do not replace exports. The private Site remains online to preserve authentication; offline mode belongs to the public version.
